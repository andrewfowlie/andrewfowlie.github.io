Dreaming is for free

August 26, 2026 — Andrew Fowlie

Read a heartbreaking story about a landslide at landfill site that killed 30 people in Guinea. Was appalled to realize that these occurrences are so common in the global south that they have their own Wikipedia entry. What an awful metaphor for the world we've created. Don't let anyone tell you a better world isn't possible.
